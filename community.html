ENGAGEMENT POLICY

Participation with purpose and trust.

These are proposed community standards for adoption and implementation as moderation tools are developed.

01

Respect everyone

No harassment, hate speech, threats, personal attacks or discrimination against passengers, workers or operators.

02

Protect privacy

Do not post passports, phone numbers, private addresses, faces or identifiable case details without proper consent.

03

Share responsibly

Label personal experiences as experiences, distinguish allegations from findings and link to reliable sources.

04

Constructive participation

Focus on education, support and lawful solutions; no coordinated harassment, brigading or retaliatory campaigns.

05

Transparent moderation

Use published rules, proportionate actions, documented decisions and a fair appeal process when a forum launches.

06

Safe volunteering

Role-based training, safeguarding contacts, conflict declarations and clear limits on representing SafeJourney.

Community governance proposal: Local facilitators → chapter moderators → independent ethics review for serious concerns. Community representatives should be selected through transparent processes, with regular feedback and review.
